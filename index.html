Frequently asked questions

How can I enquire about an appointment?

Tap "Enquire on WhatsApp" or call +91 70678 61394. Appointments are confirmed only by the clinic.

Where is the clinic located?

Dr. Matkar Hospital, Hingna Road, Mahajanwadi, Wanadongri, Nagpur, Maharashtra 441110. Use "Get Directions" to open Google Maps.

Which physiotherapy services should I ask about?

Ask about the area closest to your need, such as pain and mobility support, orthopaedic rehabilitation, or recovery after surgery. The clinic can tell you what it offers.

What should I share when requesting an appointment?

Your name, the area of concern, how long it has bothered you, any recent surgery or doctor's advice, and a convenient time to visit.

How can I confirm clinic timings?

Timings can differ between listings, so please message or call the clinic to confirm before you visit.
